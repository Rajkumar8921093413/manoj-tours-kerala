Clear & Transparent Policies

Common Booking Questions & Policies

Answers to key questions regarding check-in timings, pricing basis, swimming pool rules, jeep safari routes, and cancellation policies.

Standard check-in time is 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM and check-out time is 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is flexible upon request subject to room availability. Complimentary secure private car/bus parking and free Wi-Fi are provided at all properties.

Room tariffs (₹2,000 to ₹7,500) are charged on a Per Room / Per Night basis for base occupancy (2 adults). In contrast, all-inclusive Tour Packages (such as our ₹900, ₹1,500, ₹2,000, or ₹2,500 packages) are priced on a Per Person (Per Head) basis and include food, accommodation, campfire, and jeep safari. Rates vary by season and must be reconfirmed on WhatsApp.

Swimming pool access is available exclusively at our Swimming Pool Properties (₹5,000 & ₹6,000) during designated daytime and twilight resort hours, adhering to safety guidelines. Authentic home-cooked Kerala breakfast and dinner are included in package plans or freshly prepared on order for room bookings.

4x4 off-road safaris generally last 2.5 to 4.5 hours depending on the chosen route (e.g., Muruganpara Peak, Bhramaram Point, or Irachalpara Waterfalls). Doorstep pickup is provided from Marayoor and Kanthalloor stays. Because off-road trails wind through Western Ghats mountain ridges, specific stops may adjust based on weather or local access rules.

Fenced sandalwood forest reserves and trekking routes inside Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary (such as Thoovanam Trek) require official Forest Department tickets and authorised guides, which we coordinate for you. Wildlife sightings at Anakulam or forest borders occur in natural habitats and are subject to wild animal movements.

All bookings are confirmed directly in writing via WhatsApp upon a small advance reservation token payment (via GPay / PhonePe / Bank Transfer). Date changes or cancellations are accommodated based on advance notice and property terms shared at the time of reservation.
